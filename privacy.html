Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information Azen may process, why it is used, and how it is handled while providing its Discord bot features.

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

01

Information We May Process

Azen may process limited Discord-related information when necessary to provide features requested by a server or user.

Discord IDs Server IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, role IDs and other Discord identifiers where required by a feature.
Server Configuration Settings and preferences configured by server administrators.
Command Information Information required to process commands and return their requested results.
Moderation Information Limited event or message information where an enabled moderation or security feature requires it.
02

How Information Is Collected

Information may be received through Discord's APIs and events when Azen is installed in a server or when a user interacts with the bot.

The exact information processed depends on which Azen features are enabled and how the server has configured the bot.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide Azen's requested features.
  • Store server configuration and preferences.
  • Execute commands and automation.
  • Provide moderation and security features.
  • Detect abuse, spam or malicious activity.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of Azen.
04

Data Storage

Information required for Azen's features may be stored in databases or other infrastructure used to operate the bot.

Important: Azen should only store information that is reasonably necessary for the features being provided.

Do not use Azen to submit passwords, authentication tokens, payment card details, or other highly sensitive credentials.

05

Sharing of Information

Azen does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers that are necessary to operate Azen.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect Azen, its users or its infrastructure.

06

Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant Azen feature, maintain configuration, resolve disputes, protect the service or comply with legal obligations.

If Azen implements specific retention periods for particular types of data, those periods should be documented here.

07

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled by Azen.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

08

Data Requests

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights relating to access, correction or deletion of personal information.

For privacy or data-related requests, contact the Azen team using the contact information provided below.

09

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Azen's features, infrastructure or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page together with its effective date.
